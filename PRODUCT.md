# PRODUCT.md — Weople

## Users
- Primary: People who hold many relationships and intend to follow through, but lose the right person or promise from working memory before acting. Founders, community builders, operators juggling commitments across professional and personal worlds.
- Secondary: An AI agent (ChatGPT via WebMCP) that reasons over structured relationship data and surfaces connections the human has not yet connected.

## Product Purpose
Weople is a relationship-intelligence system for a specific failure mode: intended follow-through falls out of memory. The site holds structured data — people, timing, evidence, commitments, interventions, authority policy. ChatGPT reads the same state through WebMCP tools and notices connections across people, commitments, timing and evidence. The human retains authority over any action that affects another person. Tagline: not a pipeline, a living personal map.

## Brand
- Name: weople — lowercase serif wordmark with terracotta mark (w)
- Genre: Editorial — warm, human, anti-pipeline
- Voice: Quiet, precise, human. No sales copy, no pipeline metaphors.
- Visual anchors: warm cream paper (oklch 97.5% 0.010 92), terracotta accent (oklch 56% 0.150 40), Fraunces display + Source Sans 3 body, hairline rules, 5% accent cap.

## Tone
Calm and deliberate. Short sentences. Evidence stays separate from inference. Commitments are unresolved until acted on. Snooze means timing, not rejection.

## Anti-references
- No CRM pipeline boards, kanban columns, or funnel visuals
- No navy-and-gold fintech, no neon dark-mode, no SaaS gradient hero-metric blocks
- No glassmorphism, no gradient text, no side-stripe card accents, no identical icon-grid cards
- No charisma copy or growth-hacker urgency

## Strategic Principles
1. Fact vs inference stays inspectable — an inference never becomes a fact through use
2. Human authority over introductions and personalized communication is non-negotiable
3. Interventions cite evidence and provenance; timing corrections are explicit (snooze)
4. Shared state: site and agent read/write the same workspace (WebMCP), not a simulation
5. Synthetic demo with deterministic reset — no real PII, dates relative to today

## Surfaces
- Today — 1-col ledger: Worth your attention, Timing corrected, Context not a queue
- People — index: asymmetric 3-col grid (first card spans 2), person cards
- Person — 2-col document: facts / hypotheses / commitments + aside (cadence, related people, agent-surfaced)
- Policy — charter: authority boundaries, global + per-person overrides
- Diagnostics — modal: WebMCP tool registration, last mutation
